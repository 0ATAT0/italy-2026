🏛️ Pompeii book now

09:00–12:303.5 hrs€20–30 pp by circuit

Timed, named tickets (ID checked at the gate) on Vivaticket — the official platform since March. Morning slots for an August Saturday sell out; book the 9:00 entry now. 20,000/day cap.

The route (info-board-light): Forum → Stabian Baths → Lupanar → House of the Vettii (the best frescoes on site) → Via dell'Abbondanza → Garden of the Fugitives casts → out via the amphitheatre if legs allow → Villa of the Mysteries last, it's by the exit toward the station and its dining room is the single best painted space in the Roman world.

Tip — water fountains are dotted through the site; bring a bottle to refill. Infopoint: +39 081 8575347.

🏛️ Herculaneum book now

14:15–16:00~1.5–2 hrs€16 pp

A quarter Pompeii's size, twice the preservation: the pyroclastic flow carbonised wood instead of crushing it, so you get two-storey houses, beams, doors, even furniture. Don't miss the House of Neptune and Amphitrite mosaic, the Samnite House, and the boathouses where 300 skeletons were found mid-flight. Many rate it above Pompeii; you'll be able to argue both sides by dinner.

🏺 MANN — the third buried city

17:00–18:15metro L1/L2 or 15-min cab~€20 pp

Everything great was removed from Pompeii and Herculaneum, and it's all here: the Alexander Mosaic, the Farnese Hercules and Bull, the bronzes from the Villa of the Papyri, the Secret Cabinet. Ninety minutes on the mosaics, frescoes and Farnese floor closes the loop on the whole day. Closed Tuesdays (fine — you're here Saturday); open into the evening.

🏛️ MAR Positano — the Roman villa

PMunder Santa Maria Assuntaguided, ~30 min

Directly beneath the dome by the beach: a maritime villa buried under 11 m of ash by the same 79 AD eruption as Pompeii, found in 2003, opened 2018. Ferragosto exit

Saturday 15 August · Positano → NAP · BA to Heathrow 19:40

This is Italy's peak holiday date. The coast road is a car park and Positano holds its own procession. Whatever is chosen below gets booked immediately — it's the highest-risk gap left in the plan.

⛴️ Option A — ferry + taxi decide

dep ~12:30port ~13:50 · NAP ~14:30~€31 pp + taxi

Lucibello's ~12:30 (or NLG's ~10:55) sailing to Beverello, then 25–35 min taxi to the airport — city-side traffic is normal; the chaos is all on the coast road. Lands you ~5 hours before the flight: long wait, zero risk. Do not plan around the last sailings (~15:40/16:24): a Ferragosto cancellation there leaves no road fallback that makes 19:40.

🚐 Option B — private van, whole group decide

dep 13:00–13:30budget 3 h+~€180–280/vehicle

With more of you going back, one van door-to-door beats per-head ferry tickets and handles the luggage mountain. The honest picture: SS163 on Ferragosto is the worst road day of the year — leaving by 13:30 with a 3-hour budget still arrives ~16:30 for 19:40, but there is no slack for a real jam. Pickup is at the ZTL edge (Piazza dei Mulini area) — porter the bags down, 10 min.

Split option: heavy luggage + volunteers in the van, the impatient on the ferry.

✅ The call to make

Ferry = certain but dull. Van = comfortable but exposed on the one day the road is guaranteed awful. With a 19:40 departure both clear the bar on paper; the van's failure mode costs a flight, the ferry's costs an afternoon. Need: final headcount, luggage count, and the family's risk appetite — then it gets booked same day.

The Odyssey thread

Timely, with the film just out. Everything below is walkable or visible from the itinerary.

Naples is a Greek city

Founded ~470 BC as Neapolis — "new city" — by Greeks from Cumae, on the older settlement of Parthenope: the siren who drowned herself when Odysseus sailed past unharmed, washing ashore where the city rose. Neapolitans are still partenopei; so is the football club. The street grid you'll walk Friday night is the Greek grid — Spaccanapoli is the ancient decumanus inferior.

See it: the excavated city walls in Piazza Bellini (with a spritz, Fri + Sat nights) · the Greek agora under San Lorenzo Maggiore on Via dei Tribunali · the Greek collections at MANN (Sat 17:00).

Positano faces the Sirens

Li Galli, the three islets straight off Spiaggia Grande, are the Sirenusai — the canonical home of Homer's Sirens, stated as plain geography by Strabo two thousand years ago. Archipelago's old name: Le Sirenuse. At Punta Campanella, the peninsula tip facing Capri, legend holds that Odysseus himself founded the temple of Athena after getting past them tied to his mast.

Do it: the sunset cruise circles the islands with a swim stop — as close to the Sirens as the law allows (private islands; Nureyev was the last owner).

Sunset cruise

The 79 AD thread ties it together

Pompeii and Herculaneum on Saturday; their contents at MANN that evening; and under Positano's own church, the MAR villa — buried by the same eruption, eleven metres down, glass walkway over the frescoes. Same week of history, four sites, one trip.

Virgil watches over both ends

He lived and wrote the Georgics in Naples, set Aeneas' descent to the underworld at Cumae and Lake Avernus just up the coast (the Sibyl's cave is real and visitable, but it's a half-day — next trip), and is buried at Piedigrotta. His epitaph: "Mantua bore me, Calabria took me, now Parthenope holds me."

And your instinct about the philosophers: Pythagoras taught at Croton, far down in Calabria — but the Eleatic school (Parmenides, Zeno of the paradoxes) sat at Elea/Velia on the Cilento coast, two hours south of your beach. Closest philosophy gets to the sun loungers.

Essentials

Heat rhythm — outdoor sites 9:00–12:00, AC or sea 12:00–17:00, everything else after. Same logic as Rome, hotter pavement.

Cash — €40–60/day: market stalls, porters, beach kiosks, taxis with "broken" card readers. Bancomat ATMs attached to banks.

Taxis — Naples taxis: ask for the tariffa predeterminata (fixed posted fares) or insist on the meter. Positano ZTL: cars stop at Piazza dei Mulini, porters take it from there.

Trains — Circumvesuviana: front-worn bags, zipped phones. Campania Express when it fits the timetable.

Ferragosto week — some Naples independents close 15–25 Aug; irrelevant for your Naples dates, relevant for anything you try to book on the coast that week. Check Instagram, not Google hours.

Emergency — 112. UK EHIC/GHIC works in Italy.
